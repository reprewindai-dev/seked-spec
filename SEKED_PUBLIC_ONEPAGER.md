# SEKED

## Measure Your State. Get Your Directive. Move.

---

### The Problem

"How do you feel?" is a useless question.

Words like "tired" or "motivated" mean different things to different people. They don't tell you what to do next.

### The Solution

**SEKED replaces words with ratios.**

Instead of describing how you feel, you measure 5 things on a 0-9 scale:

| Measure | Question |
|---------|----------|
| **E** Energy | How much can I output right now? |
| **R** Resistance | How much is pushing back? |
| **C** Clarity | How clearly can I see what matters? |
| **D** Drive | How strongly am I pulled forward? |
| **S** Stability | How solid is my foundation? |

---

### The Formula

```
SEKED = (Energy + Drive) / (Resistance + 1)
```

That's it. One number that tells you everything.

---

### The Directives

| Your SEKED | What To Do |
|------------|------------|
| **Below 1.0** | **HALT** — Stop. You're running on empty. |
| **1.0 – 2.0** | **GRIND** — Slow, sustainable progress only. |
| **2.0 – 4.0** | **EXECUTE** — Build. Ship. Do. |
| **Above 4.0** | **EXPAND** or **SCALE BACK** — High output zone, but check your foundation. |

---

### How To Use It

**Daily (60 seconds):**

1. Rate E, R, C, D, S (0-9)
2. Calculate: (E + D) / (R + 1)
3. Read your directive
4. Do it. No debate.

**Example:**

```
Today: E=6, R=3, C=7, D=5, S=8

SEKED = (6 + 5) / (3 + 1) = 11 / 4 = 2.75

Directive: EXECUTE
```

You have capacity. You have clarity. Go build.

---

### Why It Works

The ancient Egyptians built pyramids using **seked** — a ratio that told workers exactly how steep to make each layer. No interpretation. No guessing.

4,500 years later, those structures still stand.

SEKED applies the same principle to you.

**Same input → Same output → Clear action**
