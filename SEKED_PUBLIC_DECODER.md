# SEKED

## A Ratio-Based System for Human Operating States

---

### What It Is

SEKED is a language for measuring internal state without words.

Before angles existed, ancient Egyptians built pyramids using **seked** — a ratio that measured slope as "horizontal run per unit of vertical rise."

No degrees. No interpretation. Just numbers that workers could follow to build structures that still stand 4,500 years later.

SEKED (the system) applies this principle to human internal states.

Instead of asking "How do you feel?" — which requires words, interpretation, and varies by person — SEKED asks: **"What's your ratio?"**

---

### How It Works

**Step 1: Measure 5 primitives (0-9 each)**

| Symbol | Question |
|--------|----------|
| **E** (Energy) | How much can I output right now? |
| **R** (Resistance) | How much is pushing back against me? |
| **C** (Clarity) | How clearly can I see what matters? |
| **D** (Drive) | How strongly am I pulled toward my goal? |
| **S** (Stability) | How solid is my foundation? |

**Step 2: Compute your SEKED ratio**

```
σ = (E + D) / (R + 1)
```

**Step 3: Get your directive**

| σ Value | Directive |
|---------|-----------|
| < 1.0 | **HALT** — Stop. Recover. |
| 1.0–1.99 | **GRIND** — Sustainable slow progress. |
| 2.0–3.99 | **EXECUTE** — Build. Ship. Do. |
| 4.0+ | **EXPAND** or **SCALE BACK** — High output or reduce load. |

**Step 4: Do it.**

### The Math

Three ratios power everything:

**SEKED (σ)** — Overall capacity
`(E + D) / (R + 1)`

**Clarity Index (CI)** — Decision readiness
`C / (R + 1)`

**Structural Integrity (SI)** — Foundation strength
`S / ((E + D) / 2)`

All three must be healthy for full execution.
